How it works

  1. Everyone puts their wrapped gift in the pile and draws a number.
  2. Player 1 opens any gift from the pile.
  3. Each next player either opens a new gift or steals one that’s already been opened.
  4. If your gift gets stolen, you open a new one or steal from someone else, but you can’t steal it right back.
  5. A gift is locked once it’s been stolen three times. Player 1 gets one final steal at the very end.
Plot twist 😈

At the end, we all vote on the worst gift of the night, and it goes right back home with the person who brought it. Choose wisely!
